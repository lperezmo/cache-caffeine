# Privacy

cache-caffeine collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

- It runs inside Claude Code on your computer. It has no server, no analytics and no telemetry.
- The only prompts it sends are the poke message and the wake prompt you set (or their defaults), into your own Claude Code session, like any prompt you type.
- Its settings (the poke message, the wake prompt, timings, a booked wake) are kept in Claude Code's own per-plugin store on your computer.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lperezmo/cache-caffeine/issues
